### Pages
- **5K column** on every page with ratings: rankings, race, school, athlete tables, compare, predictions, recruiting, course boards. Ratings are quoted as a track 5K (middle school: 3200 m).
- **Athlete header B**: Fast / Typical / Hard course words (cut-offs taken from the real difficulty spread), fewer record badges.
- **Rankings**: 3 filters inline plus "More filters". One public rating scale, with an "Advanced" toggle.
- **Nav**: Tools menu and "For coaches". **Predictions** opens with this week's meets and "Who wins state?".
- **How we did**: forecasts are frozen before each XC meet and scored after it. See `/recaps` and `/meet/recap/xc/<id>`.
- **State odds**: `/state-odds`, `/state-odds/<st>`. A season simulation of state qualifying and state-meet odds.
- **Record books**: `/records`, `/records/<st>` (state all-time lists, course records by era, on this day), and `/school/<name>/records`.
- **NCAA projection**: `/ncaa`, `/ncaa/<div>/<gender>`. Regionals, at-large selection and nationals odds for D1, D2 and D3.
- **Uploads and suggested fixes**: `/account/uploads`, `/account/fixes`. Nothing goes live until you approve it at `/account/admin/uploads` or `/account/admin/fixes`. Approved uploads get linked and rated, and grade/school/detach pins apply on Approve.
- **Usage counts and error log** (private, no IPs): `/account/status/usage`, `/account/status/errors`.
- **SEO**: canonical rules, titles built from real facts, JSON-LD (no more 500 for an athlete with no school), sitemap and IndexNow fixes.

### Data and model
- **Sitemap**: race pages from the last 2 seasons only (`RACE_YEARS=2`). `/records` pages are now listed.
- **Steeplechase**: abbreviations such as "3000mSC" and "3000 S/C" no longer count as flat 3000 m races. This needs the pipeline re-run below.
- **Watchdog** (step 13k, on by default): a nightly data-quality report. It only reports; it never changes data.
