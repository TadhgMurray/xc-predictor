## 1. Where the model stands (the next piece of work)

The site serves the **race model** (`XCP_PREDICT_BASIS=race` in
`/etc/xc-predictor.env`; `model/race_trees.py`, `racecast/race_model.py`,
rebuilt weekly by `deploy/race_model_weekly.sh`). Measured on frozen
forecasts (made before each race; `scripts/score_frozen_forecasts.py`):

| 2026-09-01..11-03 | race model (`pred_meet_forecast`, 252 races) | rating basis (`meet_forecast`, 1509 races, mostly before the switch) |
|---|---|---|
| median time miss | **1.84%** | 2.81% |
| bias | **+0.07%** | +1.26% slow |
| team score error | **46.1 pts** | 71.5 |
| winning team | **59%** | 51% |
| rho / place error, model order | 0.931 / 3.0 | 0.908 / 2.0 |
| rho / place error, same runners by rating alone (`--vs-rating`) | 0.928 / 3.0 | 0.908 / 2.0 |
| cov68 (should be 68%) | 83% (bands too wide) | 58% (too narrow) |

**The owner's point ("the model basically just regurgitates ratings") holds
for ORDER**: within a race the model barely beats rating order (0.931 vs
0.928, same place error). Its gains are the level (times, bias) and teams.
Agreed next step: make the order better than ratings —
- a target relative to the field (place or within-race time residual), not the absolute time;
- order features: head-to-head history against this field, course history,
  championship vs regular-season form, days since last race;
- score only on races after the training cut, keep a change only if it beats
  rating order on `--vs-rating`;
- re-fit the range calibration on the race basis (cov68 83%).

**Read before working on order: `docs/PLAN-MODEL-2026-10.md`.** Six model
types (HGB trees, LightGBM, LambdaRank, XGBoost, MLP, sequence net) all sit
at about 88.6% pairs against the ratings' 87.2%; the blend of six +0.07;
the hindsight ceiling (true form known) is about 90%; the room is August
(+6 pts) and September (+3), about none by November; course / distance /
heat / division inputs bought +0.01. Order is near what results can tell.
**So the next work is the FIELD** (only 61% of finishers listed on the race
model's frozen forecasts -- who runs moves places and team scores more than
order can) **and the range calibration** (cov68 83%).
