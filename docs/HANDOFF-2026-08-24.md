## 2. What was built today (all pushed to master)

The rebuild grew from three passes to five instruments, and three
corpus-wide evidence bugs were fixed. Every one was found by the fast
`--explain` (seconds instead of a 12-minute corpus build; caches pm +
ladder in `engine/data/rebuild_explain_cache.pkl`, written by full runs):

1. **pm scale collapse** — athletes missing from reb_pool fell to a raw
   100.0 scale, ratings ~0.1, gaps read +0.0: their evidence was silently
   deleted. Fixed with an `'__all__'` global pm row, used both for
   pool-less athletes and for pools the pm sample missed.
2. **Identity fragmentation** — person_id is stamped per ROW by linkage,
   so freshly scraped meets orphaned from their athletes' careers and read
   as history-less. Fixed with `reb_person` (athlete → person map);
   ident = COALESCE(row person, mapped person, athlete_id) everywhere.

   dates, unknown pool, the fake-elite raw floor) still skip.

## 3. THE SEQUENCING FACT THAT COST US TONIGHT'S PASS 4

`assertLabelTrustworthy` refused pass 4 at 48.8%: dist_override held
today's fresh proposals while the DB's ratings still reflected last
night's backfill of yesterday's union. The guard is RIGHT — proposals
would be wrong by exactly the label/rating ratio. The general law:

> A pass run must see a DB whose normalized_time was backfilled from the
> SAME dist_override it reads. Generate passes → apply → dump → pipeline
> → only THEN generate the next round. One backfill cannot both create
> the preconditions and consume the output.

`--skip-label-check` exists for diagnosis only; never for proposals.

## 6. Rules learned the hard way today (do not relearn)

- The old drop corpus and the backfill guards can HIDE distance errors
  from every detector; when a division looks inexplicably thin, ask the
  fast explain — it now prints normalized/rated counts, career
  histograms, drop-list membership, and the distance from every source.
- A career MEDIAN above 150 is a person-merge, not an athlete.
- pm "cancels" in ratios, never in absolute point gaps.
- The tier is part of the verdict; "clears every gate" without a tier is
  not a promise to write.
- Never bypass assertLabelTrustworthy for proposals.
