# Runbook — the owner's bug list, by Sunday 2026-09-27

Every command runs on the server from `/srv/xc-predictor`, as root.
Do them in this order: 1 is quick checks, 2 is the scrape, 3 is the long
pipeline run (overnight), 4 is the logo rescrape.

## 0. Update

    cd /srv/xc-predictor && git pull && systemctl restart xc-predictor

## 1. Quick checks (minutes)

**The race with no difficulty** (`/race/xc/280314/1116228?r=69975426`) —
says which join comes up empty: no meet row / no course name, a GPS that
does not match the canonical course, or no difficulty cell at that distance
(and how many of its runners have another rated race):

    /srv/venv/bin/python scripts/diag_race_difficulty.py "/race/xc/280314/1116228?r=69975426"

**The one-off races at +20%** — which of the engine's checks let them
through. The scan lists every race this season at +15% or more with how many
of its top half could vote (another XC race within ±30 days); a race run
through the first command shows each voter, the race it was compared with,
and how much of that reading the course kept:

    /srv/venv/bin/python scripts/diag_race_difficulty.py --scan --season 2025 --min 0.15
    /srv/venv/bin/python scripts/diag_race_difficulty.py "/race/xc/<meet>/<div>"
    grep -h 'on one race keeping' logs/*/08_golive.log | tail -3

**St. Mary's Invitational** — is it in the database, and what the queue
thinks of it:

    /srv/venv/bin/python scripts/find_meet.py "St. Mary"

If it shows `done` with 0 results (asked before results were posted) or
`no meet here`, or is not found at all, requeue it by id (the id is in the
athletic.net URL: `athletic.net/CrossCountry/meet/<ID>`):

    /srv/venv/bin/python scripts/find_meet.py <ID> --requeue

## 2. Scrape new meets (an hour or two; anet and tfrrs can run together)

    /srv/venv/bin/python scripts/queue_meets.py                 # dry run: what it will seed
    /srv/venv/bin/python scripts/queue_meets.py --source tfrrs
    NO_VPN=1 ANET_RETRY_FAILED=1 xvfb-run -a scripts/launcher.py        # failures first
    NO_VPN=1 PER_MEET_DELAY=3,6 xvfb-run -a scripts/launcher.py         # then everything due
    NO_VPN=1 xvfb-run -a tfrrs/driver/launch_tfrrs.py
    /srv/venv/bin/python scripts/queue_status.py --sample 20

What changed (commit `0704dff`): a failed fetch is now retried instead of
being written off as "no meet here"; the launcher no longer carries one
meet's answer into the next; and the seeder now re-asks meets that were
finished with 0 results and have no stored date, within the last 30,000
ids — the likely reason St. Mary's was missed.

Not during a backfill (step 05 of the pipeline).

## 3. The pipeline, with the weather refit (overnight)

**Why the refit.** Both weather models in `engine/data/` were fitted on the
grid's OLD temperatures, when sunny hours read 20–35 °C too hot. The grid was
corrected since (`recompute_apparent_temp`), the models were not, so a real
hot day read as cooler than "normal" and was docked — Arcadia 2025-04-12,
24 °C and windy, lost 0.9 rating points. As of `a43fbfd` a stale model is
refused (no weather correction at all, loudly) until it is refitted. This run
refits both, then rewrites every normalized time with them.

    . deploy/solve_env.sh
    XCP_WEATHER_FIT=1 bash deploy/run_pipeline.sh --from 4

Hours: the weather fit is the heavy stage, then the backfill and the solve.
The log prints `weather correction XC/TF ... NOT applied` if a model is
still stale — it should not after 04f. Check afterwards:

    /srv/venv/bin/python scripts/diag_row_weather.py --tf 258164858

The Arcadia row should now read as CREDITED for its weather (a multiplier
below 1 in that script's terms, i.e. rating points added).

Then whether rain races get their due (minutes; paste the tables back):

    /srv/venv/bin/python scripts/diag_weather_credit.py --since 2024-08-01

This run also carries, all merged today:

- **outliers**: flagged at a 4-point spread floor (`06d112b`), so a real
  breakout race is no longer taken off the boards; `09d_outliers.log` lists
  what was flagged
- **the 30-day bracket window**, **the pro-ability step** (05c), **the
  status counts** (17b)

After it: the rankings performance board no longer merges two runners who
tied at one meet, and the USA board keeps tfrrs (college) rows
(`06d112b`, live on restart).

## 4. Logos (Saturday)

The processing is fixed (`251610e`, `3a95217`): only background touching the
card's edge is removed, and a light mark on a dark card keeps its card (it
vanished on the white page before). A full rescrape re-processes every crest:

    /srv/venv/bin/python scripts/scrape_school_logos.py --redo --dry-run --limit 50
    /srv/venv/bin/python scripts/scrape_school_logos.py --redo --write
    systemctl restart xc-predictor

Or, only the crests the old key damaged (holes, or left mostly white):

    /srv/venv/bin/python scripts/scrape_school_logos.py --rekey --dry-run
    /srv/venv/bin/python scripts/scrape_school_logos.py --rekey --write

**A crest that is still wrong** (Oregon (OR)): pin the right image by URL —
it is fetched and processed like any other and no later scrape replaces it.
Use `--level college` (or `hs`) when two schools share the name and state:

    /srv/venv/bin/python scripts/scrape_school_logos.py --set-logo "Oregon" --state OR --level college --url "https://…/logo.png" --dry-run
    /srv/venv/bin/python scripts/scrape_school_logos.py --set-logo "Oregon" --state OR --level college --url "https://…/logo.png" --write
    # hide a crest altogether:  --url none
