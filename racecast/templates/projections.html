How this is projected

{% endif %}
  • The field is every {{ state_name }} school's best {{ per_school }} {{ gender_words|lower }} by this season's speed rating{% if division %}, for the schools on record in {{ division_label }}{% endif %}, plus any runner without a team.
  • The order is the season rating exactly as the rankings show it: each athlete's 80th-percentile race this season. Nothing is added for last year, for grade or for improvement. An athlete with one race is rated on that race and marked thin at {{ thin_races }} races or fewer; the race count is on every row.
  • Team scores use the same scoring as Predictions: five scorers and two displacers, seven entered per team. Runners without a team, and schools with fewer than five, race as individuals and take no points.
  • Estimated times put each rating on the state meet's usual course and distance. The order was decided on the rating, not the time.

What it does not know

  • Who qualifies. Sectionals, regionals and districts decide who reaches a state meet, and that is not modelled here. Every school is in this field, so scores run higher than at a real state meet; the order of the teams is the projection.
  • Injuries, rest and lineups. A team's best seven on paper is not always the seven who start.
  • Divisions come from the championship meets each school has run. A school that changed division this year may appear under its old one.
