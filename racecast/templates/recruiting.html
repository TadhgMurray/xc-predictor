Bands are quartiles of each school's own recent recruits, fastest programmes first inside each. "{{ scholarship_label }}" is the top quarter of that school's recruits, where athletic aid tends to go in DI, DII and NAIA; DIII gives none, so it reads "Top recruit" there.

Find your page or enter a time above, and the schools whose recruits you would sit among are listed here by band.

A recruit is an athlete's first college season{% if since_year %}, from the {{ since_year }} class on{% endif %}; a transfer is not a recruiting fact about the second school. The rating is the recruit's own last high-school season where Racecast has it, otherwise their first college season brought onto the high-school scale less the measured freshman-year gain. The times are what that rating converts to at a typical course or track. Schools need {{ min_recruits }} recruits to be listed; a school's page shows every one.
