Colleges Transfers How to read this

Typical recruit is the middle runner of a college's last four recruiting classes{% if since_year %} (from {{ since_year }}){% endif %}: half ran faster, half slower. Each recruit's number is their last high-school season in the sport -- the senior season, or the junior one when no senior season is on record.

The bar spans every recruit, fastest on the left; the blue tick is the typical recruit and the black marker is you. Every time is a season converted to a typical 5K cross country course, or a typical track for the 800, 1600 and 3200, so runners from hilly and flat states compare.

You: Top recruit means faster than three quarters of their recruits; Solid recruit faster than half; Recruit range inside the middle half; Walk-on range slower than most but faster than their slowest. A college needs {{ min_recruits }} matched recruits to be listed; its own page shows every one.
