How this projection works

  • Each team runs its seven best by season rating. Every regional is run {{ "{:,}".format(d.p.draws) }} times, each runner's day drawn from the measured race-to-race spread ({{ "%.1f"|format(d.p.race_sigma * 100) }}%), and scored by the cross country rules: five score, seven displace.
  • Each simulated regional is fed through the selection rules above, and nationals is then run with the field that came out. Qualify, Win and Top 3 are how often that happened.
  • The tables on the other tabs are one run of the rules on the projected regional order (each region's teams by average place), so they can disagree with the percentages for a team on the edge.
  • Head-to-head results are read from the results already in: {{ "{:,}".format(d.p.counts.races) }} races, {{ "{:,}".format(d.p.counts.wins) }} counted wins.{% if rule.method == "d2" %} {{ labels[division] }} compares two teams as a dual meet, as the manual does when other divisions' teams were in the race.{% endif %}
  • Where the manual leaves a decision to the committee, the projection uses a stated stand-in, and the at-large log says when one decided a berth.
  • Not seen: races still to come, injuries and redshirts, and teammates having good or bad days together.
