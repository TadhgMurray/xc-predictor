    Being built

    • Lineup simulator. Set a lineup, get win probability, expected score and which runner actually swings the result. Waiting on the prediction model's weights.
    • Recruiting projections. What a high schooler is likely to run in college, as a range rather than a number, and who is furthest below what their times suggest.
    • Programme development. How much athletes at a school improve per year against the national baseline at their level.
    • Saved boards and watchlists. Keep a recruiting list, and hear about it when someone on it races.

    Ratings are pool-relative: 100 sits at the mean of an athlete's own pool. A search is its own link — every filter lives in the query string, so you can send one to an assistant coach and they will see exactly what you saw.
