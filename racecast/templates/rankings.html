Positive is harder. +5% means a field runs about 5% slower here than the same field would on a typical course - and because difficulty is applied after distance is normalised, a 5k and an 8k belong in one list. Ratings already give the difference back, so a slow time on a hard course is not a slow performance.

Raw times and marks as the meet recorded them, uncorrected for course, conditions or altitude - a flat 5k beats the same effort on a hill, and a sprint, hurdle or field event has no rating at all. For a fair comparison of distance running use Ability or Performances.
