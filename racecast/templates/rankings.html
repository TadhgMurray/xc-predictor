{# ⚠ SHOWN ONLY WHEN THE SCOPE IS OPEN. The caveat is about comparing across the linkage graph's edges, which cannot happen on a USA board -- and a warning that is always up is a warning nobody reads. rankings.js toggles the hidden class off scope. #}
International comparison caveat: Athletes outside the USA sit on the far side of a thin linkage graph, so their ratings can be inflated or deflated by an unknown amount. Switch Scope back to USA only to compare like with like.
Cross-state note: A national board carries a per-state offset of up to about 9 points. Filtering to one state removes it entirely; individual races still carry noise of about 3 points.
