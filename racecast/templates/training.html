Where to get your files
  • Strava Settings → My Account → Download or Delete Your Account → Request your archive. Strava emails a zip; unzip it and pick the activities folder.
  • Garmin Garmin's account site → Data Management → Export Your Data. Or one run at a time: open it in Garmin Connect → the gear → Export Original (a .fit).
  • COROS In COROS Training Hub, open a run and export it as a .fit.
  • The watch itself Plugged in, most watches show their runs as .fit files in an Activity folder.

  • For each run: when, how far, how long, heart rate and climb.
