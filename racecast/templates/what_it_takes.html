  • Who qualified is whoever ran the state final: the runners in that race's own results. Nothing here knows the qualifying rules; it reads who got there.
  • The mark is the season rating (the one the rankings show) that {{ 100 - pct }}% of that group had reached, the {{ pct }}th percentile. The slowest and the median are shown too. The slowest one runner is not used as the mark, because one bad season or one mis-linked result would set it.
  • Top 25 and top 8 are finishing places at the state final. Many states give all-state honours to the top 25 and medals to the top 8, but not every state does; the lines are places, not each state's rule. "On the day" is what the 25th or 8th finisher actually ran.
  • The section round: a section or regional final's advancers are its finishers who ran that season's state meet. The round is listed under the state division most of its advancers ran.
  • Times turn each mark into a clock with the conversions page's own arithmetic: on the state meet's usual course where its difficulty is rated, and on a typical course over the same distance.
  • Trend is the change in the mark per season over the seasons shown.

When to doubt a number

  • A mark is marked thin when it rests on fewer than {{ thin_n }} rated runners (below that, the {{ pct }}th percentile is just the slowest one or two), when most of the race's runners have no season rating, when only one season is on record, or when most of the race's schools are on record in a different division or section than the race they ran. Some schools' division and section assignments are known to be wrong right now.
  • Which division a race belongs to comes from its title ("Boys Division 2") where the title says; otherwise from the schools in it, by majority. Races where neither works are left out.
  • Divisions change: a school moved up this year is judged against the marks of the division it is in now.
