How this works

{% endif %}

Typical is the middle recruit: half ran faster, half slower. Top quarter and most recruits by mark the fastest quarter and the slowest quarter. Every time is a season converted to a track -- cross country as the track 5K it is worth, the same 5K the athlete pages show -- so recruits from hilly and flat states compare; the rating beside it is the same season on Racecast's scale.

A recruit is an athlete's first college season. What counts is their last high-school season in the same sport -- the senior season, or the junior one when no senior season is on record; a recruit with neither is listed but not counted. A college's numbers are the plain median and quartiles of its recruits over the last four classes. Ratings are on the high-school {{ who|lower }}'s scale so they compare with your own; the first college season is shown the same way, as its HS-equivalent rating.

A transfer is the first season at a new college after seasons at another, in the same sport. Both ratings are college seasons shown as HS-equivalent ratings: the last season at the old school and the first at the new. An athlete whose history returns to a school is left out, since that is usually two spellings of one school rather than a move.
