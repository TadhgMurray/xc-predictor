How this works

{% endif %}

A recruit is an athlete's first college season; "recruited at" is their last high-school season where Racecast has it, otherwise (est.) the first college season on the high-school scale less the measured freshman gain. Ratings are on the high-school {{ who|lower }}'s scale so they compare with your own; the college-scale column is the number their college page shows.

A transfer is the first season at a new college after seasons at another, in the same sport. Both ratings are college-scale season ratings: the last season at the old school and the first at the new. An athlete whose history returns to a school is left out, since that is usually two spellings of one school rather than a move.
