What counts

  • A rating jump is a race whose speed rating beats the median of that athlete's earlier rated races this season (same level, same sport). Only athletes with {{ min_prior }} or more earlier races qualify, so a season opener is never a breakout. Ranked by points above that median. SB marks a race above every earlier one this season.
  • A new PR is a time at a standard distance faster than every earlier time by that athlete at that distance, in any season. A first race at a distance is a PR on the results page, but it is not listed here. Ranked by how much faster, as a share of the old PR. In cross country a PR is course-bound, so a fast course can make one.
  • The week is the {{ days }} days up to the newest race date on file for this level, so a quiet Monday still shows the weekend. Races flagged as outliers by the ratings are left out; a jump of {{ check_jump|int }}+ points is marked check because that size is often a mislabelled distance.
